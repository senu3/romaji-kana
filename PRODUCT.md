# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Individual writers who want to draft in romaji and let a local LLM smooth the text into natural Japanese. They are usually already in a writing flow and want the editor to stay out of the way while it handles conversion, phrasing, and small corrections.

## Product Purpose

Romaji Kana is a local-first Markdown editor for turning rough romaji input into readable Japanese. Success means users can open the app, start writing immediately, and convert text without losing momentum or context.

## Brand Personality

Craftlike, light, practical. The product should feel precise and dependable without becoming heavy, ceremonial, or overly technical.

## Anti-references

Avoid any first-run or settings experience that blocks writing. Avoid AI-tool theatrics, chat-app metaphors, and UI that foregrounds model configuration more than the document. Avoid slow-feeling transitions, unclear conversion states, and flows that make the user wait before they can type.

## Design Principles

1. Start in the document, not in setup.
2. Keep conversion feedback visible but quiet.
3. Make local model controls discoverable without letting them dominate the writing surface.
4. Preserve writing momentum through fast keyboard paths and reversible actions.
5. Favor familiar editor affordances over novel interaction patterns.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for contrast, focus visibility, and keyboard operation. Support reduced motion, avoid color-only state communication, and keep Japanese and English labels clear enough for mixed-language workflows.
